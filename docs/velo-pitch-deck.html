THE SITUATION

A forensic expert
opens a case.

Abuse material. A leak. Fraud.

She runs her analysis and reaches a finding.

Now she has to prove that finding to a court.

CASE FILE
Everything inside is private,
and most of it has to stay that way.
AND SHE FACES A TERRIBLE TRADE-OFF
Disclose the raw evidence
So everyone can verify her work — including private messages, photographs, and information that never needed to be exposed in the first place.
Keep the evidence private
And ask everyone to trust her process. The court gets a conclusion it cannot check, from a person it has to take at their word.
VELO chooses neither.
THIS ISN'T ONLY A FORENSICS PROBLEM

How do you prove that a rule was followed
without disclosing the private data
that the rule was designed to protect?

Medical records
Whistleblower reports
Compliance
Any workflow where the data is sensitive
We chose forensics because it is the sharpest version of that problem — and because it is a domain we understand deeply.

THE PRODUCT

Think of VELO as a professional wallet.

A crypto wallet
The asset is money.
It never sends your private key across the network. It sends a proof that you are authorized to act.
VELO
The asset is a sealed, verifiable case.
It never sends the evidence. It sends a proof that the finding was produced under the required rules.
QUERYING A PROFESSIONAL'S RECORD WITHOUT SEEING THE CASES
How many findings were accepted?
Under which jurisdiction?
Which audits verified the process?
Was an evidence package altered after it was sealed?
None of those questions requires exposing the underlying photographs, names or private messages.
These are examples of what that interface could report — not metrics we are claiming today. What is real today is the mechanism that makes those claims possible without disclosure.
“HOW DO YOU KNOW THE PERSON ISSUING THE FINDING IS ACCREDITED?”
Not facial recognition
A face proves that someone has a face. It does not prove that they are authorized to issue a forensic finding.
A credential
A zero-knowledge proof of membership in an accredited-experts registry. It proves the issuer is authorized without revealing who that person is.
